During our conversations with Gender Rights organisations regarding needs assessments of tech challenges – a requirement of readily-available resources and online helplines to combat online gender-based violence and harassment came up frequently. This document is an effort to collate such resources that help with takedown of harmful content and report online abuse and frauds. It also consists of preventive measures such as safer alternatives of communication and a digital literacy guide.

**Disclaimer:** We have collated these resources but have not used all of them ourselves, and they have not been formally evaluated. We share them in the hope that they will be useful to you, and we encourage you to check details before relying on them.

1. **Combating online Gender-based Violence**  
   1. [STOPNCII](https://stopncii.org/): Digital takedown of Non-consensual imagery   
   2. [Meri Trustline](https://ratifoundation.org/meri-trustline/) by Rati Foundation: Tech support with takedown of content, mental health counselling, and legal support to file charges.   
   3. [Responsible Netism](https://responsiblenetism.org/programs/): educational modules on online safety and cybersecurity, resources for reporting online abuse and cyber incidents.  
   4. [Tech Sakhi](https://techsakhi.in/) by POV: helpline for women, children and queer/trans folks supporting them through online incidents.   
        
2. **Reporting mechanisms**  (Reporting to cyber helplines may not be easy and you may face language barriers. In this case, document the case in the language you are best able to express and then use AI platforms like ChatGPT or Claude for translation purposes and localising reporting in your regional language.)  
   1. [National Cyber Cell](https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Accept.aspx)   
   2. [Sahyog](https://sahyog.bihar.gov.in/Sahyog/Default.aspx) (Specific to Bihar) 

3. **AI Policy drafting**  
   1. Tattle AI Use Policy Drafting Methodology: [https\://tattle.co.in/blog/2026-03-16-Tattle-AI-Policy/](https://tattle.co.in/blog/2026-03-16-Tattle-AI-Policy/)   
   2. [Tattle’s AI Policy](https://tattle.co.in/blog/2026-03-16-Tattle-AI-Policy/)  
        
4. **Basic digital literacy in English and Hindi**  
   1. [Digital Sakshar](https://digitalsakshar.com/CompetencySelection?moduleid=MOD244): Short videos to build your understanding of the internet and do basic tasks like emailing, surfing, accessing Word and joining social media. (Disclaimer: it is in Marathi)  
   2. [Citizen Digital Foundation Litt](https://litt.citizendigitalfoundation.org/lms/courses): Short digital literacy, data privacy, media literacy, online abuse courses among other subjects in Hindi and Malayalam apart from English.  
   3. [Nonprofit Ready](https://www.nonprofitready.org/courses): Short courses in English on grantwriting, program management, fundraising etc. (In case, it is useful to you.)  
5. **Safer alternative communication channels**:  
   1. [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail)/Drive:   
      1. Pros: Greater privacy through encryption of data  
      2. Con: Free account has only 1 GB data storage   
   2. [Signal Chat](https://signal.org/) for trustworthy encryption and maintaining privacy of identities in direct messaging.   
      1. Pros:   
         1. When creating groups with multiple members, nobody’s phone numbers are visible.   
         2. Stricter controls about who can reach out to you and add you to groups or DM you.    
      2. Con: It can be heavy on bandwidth. 	  
   3. [Bridgefy](https://bridgefy.me/):  
      1. Mobile application that works via bluetooth in stressful situations like in zero network areas.   
6. **Informational resources:**  
   1. Legal provisions for GBV and sex-related crimes under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita explained in simple English: [https\://webapp.safecity.in/legal\_resources](https://webapp.safecity.in/legal_resources)   
7. **Apps for cybercrime/digital safety awareness**   
   1. [Cyber Awareness Welfare Society](https://www.cyberawareness.ngo/mission): Public awareness camps and an app for raising people’s understanding about cyber security and recognising cyber crimes.   
      1. Cyber Awareness App: [https\://www\.cyberawareness.ngo/app-features](https://www.cyberawareness.ngo/app-features)  
   2. [Chayn’s DIY guide](https://www.chayn.co/guides/diy-online-safety) for Online Safety covering Sextortion, Domestic abuse, Image-based abuse, Doxing, trolling, bullying, impersonation, Cyberflashing, Sexual Abuse Material (SAM) Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)  
      

A similar and more comprehensive guide for organisations that provide feminist digital support has been created by the [Citizen Digital Foundation](https://citizendigitalfoundation.org/). Their guide can be accessed at [https\://margiindia.in/](https://margiindia.in/).

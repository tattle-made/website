## Infrastructures of Synthetic Intimate Content

![Infrastructures of Synthetic Intimate Content](https://mcusercontent.com/a9af83af1f247ecc04f50ad46/images/19681311-f760-1a52-be3f-8f573f9900a5.png)

We spent four months investigating the infrastructure powering Generative AI’s role in creating Non-Consensual Intimate Images (NCII) at scale.

The investigation was published on Decode and can be read [here](https://www.decodeinternet.in/life/investigation/desi-faces-foreign-servers-inside-the-ai-economy-undressing-women-31691).

We also sat down with ML researchers, policy researchers, and lawyers to discuss ways to reduce harms spread across internet infrastructure. A policy document is forthcoming.

We will also present at IndiaFOSS in September on the role of open source technologies in non-consensual synthetic imagery.

# Project Updates

## Deepfakes Analysis Unit

![Deepfakes Analysis Unit](https://mcusercontent.com/a9af83af1f247ecc04f50ad46/images/05c4ab3d-cdf3-2bf3-bb52-bbe09d123a39.png)

Deepfakes Analysis Unit is a collaboration with Trusted Information Alliance (TIA), where citizens can share video and audio through a WhatsApp chatbot and receive information about AI manipulations.

The data collected over the last two years is now publicly accessible through the DAU verification platform.

## Anno.Milli

Anno.milli is an open-source annotation platform built with the NCBS team.

It enables public annotations on archival material while supporting archival standards like EAD, making it useful for archives worldwide.

**Related Links**

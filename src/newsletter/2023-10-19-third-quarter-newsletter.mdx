## Product Updates

Both Viral Spiral and Uli are undergoing continuous iterative improvements

Viral Spiral hosts biweekly playtest sessions to introduce the game to new audiences and get their feedback. Sign up [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeqA61r5WQLz0G0XQGO58AnkRqLfBOB6liSDBaD6r-u_h74Kg/viewform "Come and test out an educational game!") for a chance to try the game out with the Tattle team.

Uli's slur crowdsourcing feature are being externally tested in partnership with Point of View and Nazariya. We've also been doing sessions with Feminism in India, and Chambal Media Private Limited (Khabar Lahariya) to understand how the browser plugin could help their flows better. You can see the latest Uli newsletter update [here](https://uli.tattle.co.in/blog/making-of-August-2023 "Uli").

Our in classroom game about image manipulation for Factshala Innovation Lab is taking shape and readying a public release.

Last year we worked with GitHub to understand how they could release data from public activity for international development, public policy and economics research. The work was formally released in September through, the [GitHub Innovation Graph](https://innovationgraph.github.com/ "GitHub Innovation Graph"). You can read more about the release, and a white paper based on Tattle's report on the [GitHub blog](https://github.blog/2023-09-21-announcing-the-github-innovation-graph/ "Announcing the GitHub Innovation Graph").

Also, Feluda has been [recognised](https://app.digitalpublicgoods.net/a/10707 "Feluda - DPGA Details") as a Digital Public Good by the Digital Public Goods Alliance, an UN-affiliated consortium.

## Talks and Publications

Alongside our regular blogging, Tattle and our work has been represented at different forums -

- **Study Published in Misinfo Review:** Our joint work with [Monk Prayogshala](https://tattle.co.in/products/gftw/ "Tattle -") on incentives for sharing accurate content was [published](https://misinforeview.hks.harvard.edu/article/does-incentivization-promote-sharing-true-content-online/ "Does incentivization promote sharing “true” content online? | HKS Misinformation Review") in Misinfo Review. This research was [funded](https://community.interledger.org/interledger/the-grantee-report-bf2 "The Grantee Report") by the Interledger Foundation.

- **Accessing Democracy 2023:** Denny spoke at [FLAME University](https://sites.google.com/flame.edu.in/accessingdemocracy/home "Accessing Democracy 2023") about his current focus - Technological Interventions against Online Harms. He talked about Feluda and advocated that as online platforms are increasingly closing API access, civic action in the form of data crowdsourcing is needed to effectively study and respond to misinformation.

- **Open-source AI - A Nebulous Concept Bearing a Heavy Weight:** In this [article](https://www.medianama.com/2023/09/223-open-source-ai-nebulous-concept-bearing-heavy-weight/), the associations between and current understandings of terms like öpen-source AI, scraping and some problematic use-bases of AI are explained. It relays that defining the responsibility/liability of AI is far too complex an issue for one unified framework.

- **To Think of Interdisciplinarity as Intercurrence:** The [paper](https://jimis.episciences.org/paper/view/id/8915 "To think of interdisciplinarity as intercurrence: Or, working as an interdisciplinary team to develop an ML tool to tackle online gender-based violence and hate speech") borrows the concept of intercurrence from Orren & Skowronek (1996) and reorients it to explicate the practice of interdisciplinary research by reflecting on the working of an interdisciplinary team consisting of researchers and activists from the field of computer science and social sciences involved in developing.

- **Keynote on Civic Tech and Alternative Visions of Tech:** Tarunima delivered a keynote address at the annual symposium organized by the Australian Research Council Center of Excellence on Automated Decision-Making and Society. Her [talk](https://tattle.co.in/blog/a-new-thing-under-the-sun "Tattle -") explored the promise but also challenges for alternative visions for tech.

- **CCG Tech Podcast:** In this [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2P3_WIaBJhE "Ep.06 Fake News, Fact Checking and Beyond: The Misinformation Ecosystem in India"), Nidhi and Shashank from the CCG team speak to Tarunima about the misinformation ecosystem in India. They discuss what misinformation is, the depth of the problem in India, how it spreads over channels like WhatsApp, and how we can guard against misinformation as we consume content online.

- **Tattle's Blog Enhancement:** We've taken steps to better organize the wealth of information on our [blog](https://tattle.co.in/blog/ "Tattle -"). With thematic labels, we're making it easier for you to access and explore the insightful content we've been curating including our Twitter threads which end up getting published on our blog as well. You can now keep up with our blog updates through RSS. Old school has its place :-).

In the last quarter of 2023, we're focusing on releasing 'Facts', scaling Viral Spiral, and releasing the feature to crowdsource data through Uli. We're glad to have Aatman, Siddharth, and Kaustubha join the Tattle family in this phase of intense work. Aatman has joined as a full-stack developer and is supporting Viral Spiral and Uli. Siddharth is working on data governance frameworks for the Uli data. Kaustubha is a volunteer helping build the developer community for Uli.

That is a wrap on this edition of the newsletter. Take care and have a fantastic and festive end to the year!

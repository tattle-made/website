# Viral Spiral Pilot in Mumbai

![Viral Spiral Pilot](https://mcusercontent.com/a9af83af1f247ecc04f50ad46/images/5c7c382a-51dc-c119-1ff5-1c80d9ebbcd0.jpg)

[Viral Spiral](https://tattle.co.in/products/viral-spiral/) is our multiplayer card game that you play with your friends to simulate social media behaviours and experience motivated reasoning and cognitive biases in action. You earn clout by sharing news with affinities and prejudices in them. The first person to get **10 clout before the world gets polarized** wins.

Since all storytelling dreams come true in Mumbai, this September we went there to meet real board gamers and conduct guided playtests.

We met them all—cynics, enthu-cutlets and those who came "because my friend invited me."

One interesting feedback was that gamers did not want to read the card text and source text. This is very different from the media literacy and storytelling community, who actually enjoy reading those parts.

Gamers mainly wanted to compare card scores quickly so they could maximize points. This highlighted the challenge of balancing **media literacy** with **fun gameplay**.

We still strongly believe reading is an important part of the experience—we're definitely **not contributing to the global decline in reading rates!**

# From Uli to AI Safety

The Uli project demonstrated that a participatory, expert-led approach was effective in building good datasets for online safety.

The same approach we used to crowdsource the slur list and the Uli dataset also helped us build **[LLM/AI Safety Benchmarks for MLCommons](https://tattle.co.in/products/ml-commons-safety-benchmark/).**

![MLCommons](https://mcusercontent.com/a9af83af1f247ecc04f50ad46/images/0cb40f23-5b17-425c-f51e-a356ec7f3c68.png)

*While many LLMs claim to be multilingual, their performance across languages varies significantly. Robust evaluations can reveal hidden vulnerabilities.*

We're happy to be working with **Tech4Dev** on AI safety guardrails for nonprofits participating in their AI Cohort Program.

The cohort includes seven nonprofits building AI-powered solutions such as chatbots and information summarization systems.

We introduced them to frameworks such as the MIT AI Risk Repository and created a proof of concept demonstrating guardrails for [chatbot applications](https://github.com/tattle-made/llamaguard-uli).

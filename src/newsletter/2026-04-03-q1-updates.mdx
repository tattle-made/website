Welcome to the Quarterly Update Newsletter from Tattle. Here’s a rundown on what we’ve been up to in Quarter 1 of 2026.

## Highlights

- We co-hosted 2 events on AI safety at the India AI Impact Summit in New Delhi.
- We are close to making the data collected by the Deepfakes Analysis Unit available for public access.
- We conducted 12 Uli workshops over 3 days at a College in Coimbatore.
- We built guardrails for Personally Identifiable Information, Indian Slurs and Gender Assumption Bias.
- Uli has been selected in UNICEF’s [Femtech Ventures](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/femtech-cohort-1) cohort.

---

## Uli and UNICEF Femtech Ventures Cohort

Over the years, datasets and ML models created for Uli have been used by researchers, civil society and platforms to research or moderate online gender based violence.

However, the social media users who are on the receiving end of online abuse have not been able to use Uli directly so far.

Through the Femtech venture fund program we are testing a business model around consensual data sharing and social media monitoring.

[Read more about the Cohort](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/femtech-cohort-1)
